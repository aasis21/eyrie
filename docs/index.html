What you get

  • your command — opens Copilot in your HQ from any terminal; enable brings HQ tools into a project
  • eyrie extension — activate, peek and search any project's skills, prompts, agents and instructions
  • session messaging — separate Copilot sessions on your machine can coordinate
  • remote agents — delegate to A2A agents you approve
  • lore — optional Markdown memory: Copilot learns your corrections and preferences and loads them every session
  • dreaming — say “dream” (or schedule it overnight) to learn from recent sessions; see an undoable digest next time
  • corpus & relay — keep designs and research you ask to save; hand off work across machines and sessions
  • skills — falconry (plan until “aloft”), preen (code audit), tick-me, session communication, workflow discovery, dev-workflow (commit or PR, learned per project), relay, corpus
  • web-capture — sanitized screenshots and raw screen recordings of web UIs (Playwright MCP)
  • product-video — plan, record and produce narrated product videos with Clueso (optional Clueso MCP)
